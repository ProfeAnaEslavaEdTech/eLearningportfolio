I design learning experiences that people actually finish — engaging and easy to measure. For 20+ years I've turned complex, highly technical topics into clear, structured courses for universities, large companies and product teams alike. Native Spanish with C1 English, I design and deliver full corporate eLearning in English, aligned to the client's style guide and official accessibility guidelines.

What I bring to a team

  • Solid learning structures. ADDIE, SAM and Agile, with gamification, microlearning and realistic problem-solving that keep learners engaged start to finish.
  • Decisions based on data. SCORM and xAPI embedded in the lessons, connecting LMS data with learning analytics to improve from real results.
  • Accessible to everyone. Web accessibility and Universal Design for Learning from the first sketch, not a late compliance fix.
  • Human + AI teamwork. Humans make every teaching decision; AI only speeds up production, without sacrificing quality.
