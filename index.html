Design philosophy

Learning is engineered, not decorated.

Every project starts with the learner and the outcome — never with the tool. I map where a learner is, where they need to be, and the smallest sequence of experiences that closes the gap. Motivation, cognitive load, and accessibility are designed in from the first storyboard.

🎯

Outcomes first

Measurable objectives written before any content or activity is chosen — Bloom-aligned, tied to real performance.

🧩

Chunked & scaffolded

Information sequenced to respect working memory — progressive disclosure, one cognitive step at a time.

♿

Accessible by design (WCAG)

Built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and UDL — keyboard navigation, semantic structure, colour contrast, captions, and multiple means of representation, engagement, and expression.

🤝

Responsible AI use

AI is a transparent, disclosed co-author — accelerating production while every pedagogical and design decision stays human-directed and accountable.

📈

Evidence-driven

Iterative prototyping and review cycles — design decisions tested and revised, not assumed.

A process built for iteration

I anchor projects in the ADDIE lifecycle but run it the SAM way — in fast, iterative loops (analyse → prototype → review) rather than a rigid waterfall.

01

Analyse

Learner profile, prior knowledge, context, constraints. Define the performance gap and success criteria.

02

Design

Objectives, assessment strategy, storyboards, and interaction design documents for each level.

03

Develop

Build activities and media — interactive video, quizzes, worksheets, avatar lessons.

04

Implement

Deploy across the LMS and web, with teacher and student guides to support delivery.

05

Evaluate

Gather feedback, measure against outcomes, feed insights into the next iteration.

Frameworks in the loop: SAM for iteration · Casañ-Pitarch's Input → Processing → Output for skill progression · ESA (Engage–Study–Activate) and the CLIL 4Cs for lesson structure.

The theory behind the decisions

Good instructional design is theory made invisible. Each framework drives a concrete design choice in the courses I build.

SLA

Comprehensible Input +1

Krashen — resources pitched just above current proficiency, scaffolded by video, visuals, and AI support.

SLA

Noticing & Output

Schmidt & Swain — focused-attention tasks, then production of abstracts and spoken pitches.

Motivation

Self-Determination Theory

Ryan & Deci — designing for autonomy, competence, and relatedness through choice, mastery, and narrative.

Gamification

Kapp & Deterding

Game mechanics applied to learning: levels, XP, badges, and a boss challenge that sequence mastery.

Cognition

Cognitive Load Theory

Sweller — content chunked, extraneous load removed, visual hierarchy that guides rather than overwhelms.

Access

Universal Design for Learning

CAST — multiple means of representation, action, and engagement, with built-in accessibility controls.

Writing

Swales' Move Analysis

The rhetorical structure of research abstracts, taught progressively across levels using real texts.

Writing

Hyland's Metadiscourse

Interactive and interactional markers taught explicitly, then applied through classification games.

Evidence & analysis

Data & research.

Instructional design grounded in research. My master's thesis corpus is reimagined as an interactive, three-tab data book — modelling transparent, AI-assisted academic analysis.

📊 Interactive · Chart.js

Metadiscourse in Master's Thesis Abstracts

A three-tab web app — Corpus Data Book · Statistical Results · References — rendering real METOOL marker data, inferential statistics, and the full bibliography live in the browser. Colour-vision-validated palette, light/dark themes, downloadable workbooks.

Corpus

6 disciplines

Master's abstracts across Architecture, Engineering, Health, Arts & Humanities, Sciences, Social & Legal.

Method

Hyland (2005)

Textual & interpersonal metadiscourse markers, normalised per 1,000 tokens.

Stats

Kimi + Python

Chi-square, effect sizes, Juilland's D, Bayes factors — cross-checked with Lancaster Stats Tools.
