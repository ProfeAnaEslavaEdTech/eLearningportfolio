Chemical Spill Response SOP

A zero-error compliance microlearning course that turns a seven-step laboratory safety procedure into an accessible, measurable, LMS-ready training — built the way regulated pharma, MedTech and manufacturing teams need it: SCORM 2004, cmi5 and xAPI, ready for Cornerstone and any LRS.

Overview

Designed for people whose mistakes have consequences.

In a regulated laboratory, a spill handled the wrong way is a safety incident and a compliance deviation at once. This course trains technicians to follow the Standard Operating Procedure exactly — every step, every time — and gives supervisors the evidence that they do.

Instructional design

The method behind it.

Engineered with ADDIE, assessed to a zero-error mastery standard, and evaluated across all four Kirkpatrick levels — not a "did they click through it" completion.

ADDIE

Outcomes first

Mager-style objectives (condition, behaviour, criterion) written before any screen was built, tied to real on-the-job performance.

Assessment

Zero-error mastery gate

Critical SOP steps require 100%. A wrong choice does not advance — it returns the learner to the decision until it is right.

Evaluation

Kirkpatrick L1–L4

L1 reaction survey; L2 in-course checkpoints; L3 supervisor observation at 30/60/90 days; L4 incident-rate results.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA

Captions, audio scripts, keyboard-navigable interactions, AA contrast, and clear new-tab cues throughout.

Bilingual

English master to Spanish

Authored in English, then translated to a learner-selectable Spanish version — one language per screen, never mixed.

Transparency

EU AI Act Art. 50

AI-assisted production is disclosed; every pedagogical and compliance decision stays human-directed and accountable.

The seven steps the course trains

1

Assess

Consult the SDS or label first — never by sight, smell or touch.

2

Alert & evacuate

Warn people nearby and clear the area. People first.

3

PPE

Gloves, goggles and apron before approaching.

4

Contain

Ring with absorbent, outside-in, away from drains.

5

Correct kit

Match the kit to the chemical — dry kit for water-reactive.

6

Dispose

Labelled hazardous-waste container only.

7

Document

Complete the incident log and notify the supervisor.

Explainer

What is cmi5 — and how would you use it?

cmi5 is a modern e-learning standard: an xAPI profile that defines how an LMS launches a course and how that course reports back. It is the reliable completion and launch rules of SCORM, carried on the rich data pipe of xAPI. ADL released it in 2016 to replace SCORM for new content.

SCORM 2004

2004 — the classic
  • Runs only inside a browser, inside the LMS window
  • Tracks score, status and completion — but little about how learners performed
  • Universally supported, including Cornerstone
  • No native support for mobile, VR or offline

xAPI (Tin Can)

2013 — the data layer
  • Records any experience as actor–verb–object statements
  • Sends data to a Learning Record Store, not just an LMS
  • Tracks simulations, video, on-the-job tasks, offline, mobile
  • On its own it does not define launch or completion

cmi5

2016 — best of both
  • xAPI's rich tracking with SCORM's launch and completion rules
  • The LMS launches it and knows, reliably, when it is passed
  • Granular xAPI statements still flow to the LRS for analytics
  • Mobile- and distributed-content friendly

How this course uses cmi5 in practice

  1. Import. Upload spill-sop-cmi5.zip to the LMS (Cornerstone, or any cmi5-capable LMS) and attach its Learning Record Store.
  2. Launch. The LMS launches the course with a secure cmi5 session — it knows exactly who the learner is and when the attempt starts.
  3. Track. As the learner moves through the seven steps and the decision scenario, the course sends xAPI statements — e.g. "learner answered Step 5 correctly" — to the LRS.
  4. Gate. The zero-error logic reports passed only when every critical step is correct — a trustworthy compliance record, not just "viewed".
  5. Report. L2 checkpoint data joins L3 supervisor observation and L4 incident metrics to prove the training changed behaviour on the bench.

Which should you deploy? On Cornerstone today, SCORM 2004 4th Ed. is the safest universal choice. If your Cornerstone instance or LRS has cmi5 enabled, use the cmi5 package for far richer, step-level compliance evidence. Use the xAPI package when you want the data to live in a dedicated LRS for cross-course analytics.
