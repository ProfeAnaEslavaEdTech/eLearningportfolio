Overview

Designed for people whose mistakes have consequences.

In a regulated laboratory, a spill handled the wrong way is a safety incident and a compliance deviation at once. This course trains technicians to follow the Standard Operating Procedure exactly — every step, every time — and gives supervisors the evidence that they do.

The 7 steps the course trains

1

Assess

Consult the SDS/label first — never by sight, smell or touch.

2

Alert & evacuate

Warn people nearby and clear the area. People first.

3

PPE

Gloves, goggles and apron before approaching.

4

Contain

Ring with absorbent, outside-in, away from drains.

5

Correct kit

Match the kit to the chemical — dry kit for water-reactive.

6

Dispose

Labelled hazardous-waste container only.

7

Document

Complete the incident log and notify the supervisor.

Explainer

What is cmi5 — and how would you use it?

cmi5 is a modern e-learning standard: an xAPI "profile" that defines how an LMS launches a course and how that course reports back. Think of it as the reliable completion and launch rules of SCORM, carried on the rich data pipe of xAPI. It was released by ADL in 2016 to replace SCORM for new content.

SCORM 2004

2004 · the classic
  • Runs only inside a web browser, inside the LMS window
  • Tracks score, status, completion — but little about how learners performed
  • Universally supported (incl. Cornerstone)
  • No native support for mobile, VR, or offline

xAPI (Tin Can)

2013 · the data layer
  • Records any experience as "actor–verb–object" statements
  • Sends data to a Learning Record Store (LRS), not just an LMS
  • Tracks simulations, video, on-the-job tasks, offline, mobile
  • Powerful — but on its own it doesn't define launch or completion

cmi5

2016 · best of both
  • xAPI's rich tracking + SCORM's launch & completion rules
  • The LMS launches it and knows, reliably, when it's "passed/completed"
  • Granular xAPI statements still flow to the LRS for analytics
  • Mobile- and distributed-content friendly

How this course uses cmi5 in practice

  1. Import: upload spill-sop-cmi5.zip to the LMS (Cornerstone, or any cmi5-capable LMS) and attach its Learning Record Store.
  2. Launch: the LMS launches the course with a secure cmi5 session — it knows exactly who the learner is and when the attempt starts.
  3. Track: as the learner moves through the 7 steps and the decision scenario, the course sends xAPI statements — e.g. "learner answered Step 5 correctly" — to the LRS.
  4. Gate: the zero-error logic means passed is only reported when every critical step is correct — a trustworthy compliance record, not just "viewed".
  5. Report: L2 data (checkpoints) joins L3 supervisor-observation and L4 incident metrics to prove the training actually changed behaviour on the bench.
Which should you deploy? On Cornerstone today, SCORM 2004 4th Ed. is the safest universal choice. If your Cornerstone instance (or your LRS) has cmi5 enabled, use the cmi5 package for far richer, step-level compliance evidence. Use the xAPI package when you want the data to live in a dedicated LRS for cross-course analytics.
